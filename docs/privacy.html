Privacy Policy for Dragons TD

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Dragons TD (“the game”) is published by Dark Lamp Games Studio (“we”, “us”). Contact: dark.lamp.g@gmail.com

Short version

We do not ask for your name, email or an account. The game needs an internet connection and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as explained below.

What the game stores on your device

The game saves your progress (levels won, stars, gold, crystals, upgrades, Dragon Rider names and settings such as music, sound effects and language) in a small file on your own device. We cannot see this file. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, it is deleted.

Internet connection

The game checks that your device is online when it starts and when you return to it. This check only asks a Google address whether it can be reached; it sends none of your game data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob: an optional rewarded ad that doubles a level’s rewards, and a full-screen ad after some levels. To show, limit and measure ads, Google may collect and use: - your device’s advertising ID, IP address and general location (country or city); - device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version); - how you interact with ads (views, taps, whether a rewarded ad was finished).

Google uses this information under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the game asks for your consent before ads use personal data, and you can change your choice later from Options > Privacy settings in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

Google Play

If you download the game from Google Play, Google may collect information under its own privacy policy (for example installs, crash reports and device model). See https://policies.google.com/privacy

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the game in a way that shared personal data, contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.

Contact

Questions: dark.lamp.g@gmail.com
